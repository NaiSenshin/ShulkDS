> **Disclaimer:** No Minecraft files are included. On first launch, sign in with a Microsoft account that owns *Minecraft: Java Edition* and ShulkDS will download and set up 26.3 on your device.

## What's New

- **Distant Horizons off by default:** Turned off out of the box to save battery and heat, and can still be enabled anytime in Options > Mods.
- **16-chunk render distance:** Raised the default render distance to 16 chunks.
- **Removed tutorial popups:** Disabled the top-right tutorial hint toasts when starting a new world.

## Install

- Download **`shulkds-1.0.1-arm64-v8a.apk`** below and open it on your handheld.
- **Updating from `v1.0.0`:** Install the new APK right over your current install.
- **Updating from an older `0.8.0` test build:** Uninstall `0.8.0` first. Your worlds and settings in `/sdcard/ShulkDS` will stay intact.
